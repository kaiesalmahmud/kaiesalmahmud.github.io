Cropping intensity is the number of crops harvested from the same land in a
year. We mapped it for all 544 sub-districts of Bangladesh using MODIS NDVI
time series from 2000 to 2021, processed in Google Earth Engine.

This was one of three agricultural remote sensing projects I worked on at the
lab, using machine learning and geospatial analysis.
