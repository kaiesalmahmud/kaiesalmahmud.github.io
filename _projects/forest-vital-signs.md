Vegetation optical depth describes how much a forest canopy weakens a
microwave signal, and it is linked to the water held in the vegetation. This
project measures it with GNSS-transmissometry, using navigation satellite
signals received under the canopy, and extends the maps with airborne LiDAR.

I operate and maintain the Husky ground rover that carries the receivers, and
I helped design its gimbal payload.

The team won the 2025 IEEE GRSS Student Grand Challenge. The work was
published at IGARSS 2026.
