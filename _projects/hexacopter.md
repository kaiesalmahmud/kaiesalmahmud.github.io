The radiometer is designed to move between a stationary box and an unmanned
aircraft. For the aircraft, I assembled a custom X6 hexacopter with a Pixhawk
flight controller and designed a carbon-fiber mount for the instrument. The
smaller parts were designed in Autodesk Fusion and 3D printed.

So far the aircraft has flown with dummy payloads of up to 10 lb, and it has
completed an automated test flight at AERPAW. Flights with the radiometer on
board are the next step.

I hold an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate.
