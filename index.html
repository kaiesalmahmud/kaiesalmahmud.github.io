I build RF instruments and the data behind them.

I'm Kaies Al Mahmud, a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Georgia. I designed and built an S-band microwave radiometer on a software-defined radio, and I use it in controlled 5G interference experiments at the AERPAW testbed.
