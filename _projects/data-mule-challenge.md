Teams wrote the flight and decision logic for a drone that acts as a data
mule. The drone chooses which base station to talk to and where to fly, based
on the link quality it measures. Solutions were developed in the AERPAW
digital twin and then flown on the real testbed.

I led our team, and we tied for third place. The
measurements from all finalist teams were later published as an open dataset
in IEEE Data Descriptions.
